# CADE ESPORTS PRO LEAGUE – DIVISION 2

## CREATOR BRIEF (OFFICIAL INFORMATION)

### League Overview

CADE Esports Pro League: Division 2 (also referred to as the Pro Cup / Division 2) is the secondary professional tier of the CADE E-Soccer ecosystem, built to identify elite talent for promotion into the Elite League (Division 1). This is a national competition featuring players across Nigeria (including Abuja, Benin, Port Harcourt, Lagos, and more) competing in a structured league format.

Game: EA FC 26

Mode: Ultimate Team (FUT)

Platform: PlayStation 5

Season Duration: 7 weeks (weekend matchdays)

Division 2 Prize Pool: ₦1,300,000 

### Competitive Pathway Structure (Grassroots → Elite)

#### 1. Onile (Grassroots – Lagos Game Centers)

Hosted across 20 Local Governments in Lagos (game centers).

Match Settings: 95-rated teams

Prize Pool: ₦1,000,000 

Progression:

1st Place: Direct slot into Division 2

2nd & 3rd Place: Slots into Division 3 (Challengers Cup)

#### 2. Division 3 (Challengers Cup)

Match Settings: 95-rated teams.

Top performers can progress into Division 2.

#### 3. Division 2 (Pro League – This League)

Match Settings: FUT (not 95-rated).

Entry routes:

Qualified from Division 3

Some secured slots via paid entry (₦50,000)

Format: Single round-robin — every player faces every other player once across the season.

Promotion: Top 8 from Division 2 advance to Division 1 (Elite League), where top players are already waiting.

#### 4. Elite League (Division 1)

Starts: April.

Prize Pool: ₦3,000,000.

Goal: Top 4 players from Division 1 will represent Nigeria in the Naija vs Ghana Face-Off in June.

### Beyond the Leagues

Naija vs Ghana Face-Off (June): Top 4 from Division 1 represent Nigeria.

CAGE 26: Announced $20,000 prize pool event.

### Matchdays and Start Times (Division 2)

Division 2 is played in-studio every weekend:

Saturdays: Matches start at 10:00 AM

Sundays: Matches start at 1:00 PM

Fixtures for each weekend are posted on CADE’s official social media pages ahead of matchdays. We can send to you if need be.

### Key Rules (Critical Elements Only)

Players are required to be punctual and follow matchday operations.
