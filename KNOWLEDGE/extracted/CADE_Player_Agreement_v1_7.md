# SECTION D: TERMS & CONDITIONS OF PARTICIPATION

ARTICLE 1: ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF RULES

1.1  The Participant acknowledges that they have received, read, and fully understood the following documents, each of which forms part of this Agreement:

CADE Esports eSoccer League: Official Rulebook & Regulations (Version 1.7, Season 2025-2026)

CADE Esports: Integrity, Anti-Match Fixing & Betting Policy (Version 1.4, Season 2025-2026)

This Player Registration Agreement & Code of Conduct

1.2  The Participant agrees to comply fully with all provisions of the above documents throughout the season and any subsequent disciplinary period.

1.3  The Participant acknowledges that ignorance of any rule shall not be accepted as a defence in any disciplinary proceeding.

ARTICLE 2: ELIGIBILITY DECLARATIONS

2.1  The Participant declares that all information provided in Sections B and C of this Agreement is true, accurate, and complete. They understand that providing false information is grounds for immediate disqualification and a potential ban.

2.2  The Participant declares that they meet all eligibility requirements for the division in which they are registering, as set out in Part Two of the Official Rulebook.

2.3  The Participant declares that they are not currently serving a ban or suspension imposed by CADE Esports or any other recognised esports governing body, except as previously disclosed in writing to CADE.

2.4  The Participant declares that their EA account is in good standing and that they are the sole authorised user of that account. Account sharing or use of another person’s account in CADE competition is prohibited.

2.5  Division 1 specific: The Participant declares that their affiliated organisation is a CAC-registered legal entity and that a valid Organisation-Player Contract is in place. They further declare that they have a registered coach as required by the Rulebook.

ARTICLE 3: MATCH PARTICIPATION OBLIGATIONS

3.1  The Participant agrees to be available for ALL scheduled match days throughout the entire season without exception. Failure to appear on any match day without an approved reason is a breach of this Agreement.

3.2  The Participant agrees to arrive at the official venue ON TIME as specified in the match day schedule, regardless of when their individual match is scheduled. Being present but not checked in with the LOC representative does not constitute punctual arrival.

3.3  The Participant acknowledges and accepts the progressive late arrival sanction system set out in Section 3.8 of the Official Rulebook, including point deductions and the possibility of a season ban for repeated lateness.

3.4  The Participant agrees to dress appropriately and presentably at all official CADE events, in accordance with the Dress Code requirements in Section 3.7 of the Official Rulebook.

3.5  The Participant agrees to notify the League Operations Committee at least 2 hours in advance of any genuine inability to attend a scheduled match, and to provide supporting documentation within 24 hours.

3.6  The Participant acknowledges that repeated unexcused absences, unexplained forfeits, or suspicious patterns of non-performance may be referred to the Integrity & Disciplinary Committee.

3.7  Division 1 specific: The Participant agrees to submit their FUT squad roster to the LOC by 10:00 AM every Thursday before a match weekend. The Participant acknowledges that failure to submit may result in a forfeit for that weekend’s matches.

3.8  Division 1 specific: The Participant agrees to post at least once per week on a minimum of two (2) of the following platforms: Twitter/X, Instagram, or TikTok, positively promoting the CADE Esports eSoccer League and encouraging viewership. The Participant acknowledges that failure to comply with this obligation may result in league point deductions.

3.9  Division 2 specific: The Participant confirms that they will bring their own console, controller, and peripherals to every in-person match event, and that their equipment will comply with the BYOD rules in the Rulebook.

ARTICLE 4: INTEGRITY & ANTI-CORRUPTION OBLIGATIONS

4.1  The Participant agrees NOT to engage in, attempt, or facilitate match fixing, spot fixing, result manipulation, deliberate underperformance for corrupt purposes, or any other form of conduct prohibited under the CADE Integrity Policy.

4.2  The Participant agrees NOT to place any bet on any match in which they are a direct participant.

4.3  The Participant agrees NOT to place any bet on any CADE League match using inside information obtained by virtue of their participation in the League.

4.4  The Participant agrees NOT to disclose inside information (squad selection, health/form, withdrawal intent, or other non-public information) to any person where there are reasonable grounds to believe that person could use it for betting advantage.

4.5  The Participant agrees that if they receive ANY approach from any person seeking to arrange corrupt conduct or obtain inside information for betting purposes, they WILL report that approach to the IDC within 24 hours via the Confidential Reporting Hotline, and will preserve all communications relating to the approach.

4.6  The Participant acknowledges that failure to report a corrupt approach within 24 hours is itself a disciplinary offence under the Integrity Policy.

4.7  The Participant consents to the monitoring of their in-game performance data and, where legally permissible and a warrant or appropriate legal process is obtained, their betting account activity, for the purposes of integrity monitoring.

4.8  The Participant agrees to cooperate fully and honestly with any IDC investigation, including attending hearings, providing documents, and giving truthful testimony. Obstruction or dishonesty in an investigation will be treated as a serious aggravating factor.

ARTICLE 5: CODE OF CONDUCT

5.1  The Participant agrees to treat all fellow players, coaches, officials, volunteers, spectators, and CADE staff with respect and dignity at all times.

5.2  The Participant agrees that any public statement (spoken, written, or social media post) that brings CADE Esports, its competitions, its sponsors, or its participants into disrepute may constitute a conduct violation subject to disciplinary action.

5.3  The Participant agrees not to use offensive, abusive, racist, sexist, homophobic, or otherwise discriminatory language in any official CADE communication or at any CADE event.

5.4  The Participant agrees not to consume alcohol or controlled substances during any official CADE match event. Arriving at an event visibly impaired is a conduct violation.

5.5  The Participant agrees not to engage in, encourage, or allow cheating behaviour, including use of exploits, glitches, unauthorised software, macros, or modified hardware, in any CADE match.

ARTICLE 6: PRIVACY & DATA CONSENT

6.1  The Participant consents to CADE Esports collecting and processing their personal data (name, contact details, ID, EA account data, in-game performance data, match recordings) for the purposes of: (a) competition administration; (b) prize disbursement; (c) integrity monitoring; (d) regulatory compliance; and (e) marketing communications (which may be opted out of separately).

6.2  CADE Esports will process personal data in accordance with the Nigerian Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Data will not be sold to third parties, but may be shared with: (a) licensed betting operators for integrity purposes; (b) law enforcement bodies under legal compulsion; and (c) the EA platform as required for competition administration.

ARTICLE 7: PRIZE MONEY & FINANCIAL TERMS

7.1  Prizes will be disbursed to the bank account registered in Section B of this Agreement within SIXTY (60) working days of the season’s conclusion. The Division 1 prize pool for Season 2025/2026 is ₦3,000,000 total, distributed as shown in the table below.

DIVISION 1 PRIZE DISTRIBUTION (TOTAL POOL: ₦3,000,000)

7.2  Prizes are subject to applicable Nigerian withholding tax. CADE Esports will deduct and remit withholding tax as required by Nigerian tax law and will provide documentation to the Participant.

7.3  A Participant under investigation for or convicted of an integrity violation at the time of prize disbursement will have their prize withheld pending resolution. If found guilty, prizes relating to the affected matches or season may be permanently forfeited.

7.4  The Participant acknowledges that CADE Esports has no obligation to compensate any player for costs of participation including equipment, travel, squad development (FUT coins/cards), or other personal expenditures, unless specifically agreed in a separate commercial arrangement.

ARTICLE 8: SANCTIONS & CONSEQUENCES

8.1  The Participant acknowledges that breach of any provision of this Agreement, the Official Rulebook, or the Integrity Policy may result in one or more of the following sanctions:

Formal written warning

Match forfeit and/or suspension

Relegation or disqualification from the current season

Forfeiture of prize money

Temporary or permanent ban from all CADE Esports competitions

Public announcement of disciplinary action

Referral to Nigerian law enforcement authorities (for criminal conduct)

8.2  The Participant acknowledges that the IDC has full authority to impose these sanctions, and that the IDC’s decision may only be challenged through the formal appeals process set out in the Integrity Policy.

8.3  The Participant agrees that CADE Esports’ financial liability for any act or omission under this Agreement is limited to the return of the registration fee paid, except in cases of CADE’s proven gross negligence.

ARTICLE 9: TERM & TERMINATION

9.1  This Agreement is effective from the date of signing and remains in force for the duration of the Season 2025-2026, including any applicable disciplinary periods extending beyond the season.

9.2  CADE Esports may terminate this Agreement and a Participant’s registration immediately upon a finding of an integrity violation or serious conduct breach.

9.3  A Participant who voluntarily withdraws from the League must notify the LOC in writing. Voluntary withdrawal does not refund registration fees and the Participant remains bound by confidentiality and integrity obligations for 1 year post-withdrawal.

ARTICLE 10: ORGANISATION LIABILITY

10.1  The registered Esports Organisation affiliated with the Participant (as named in Section C) acknowledges and accepts joint responsibility for ensuring that the Participant complies with all obligations under this Agreement, the Official Rulebook, and the Integrity Policy throughout the season.

10.2  Where a Participant is found guilty of a serious integrity violation, including but not limited to match fixing, spot fixing, corrupt betting, deliberate corrupt forfeiting, or any other form of fraud connected to CADE League competitions: the affiliated organisation may be held legally liable and subject to civil action by CADE Esports, affected third parties (including licensed betting operators and sponsors), and other aggrieved parties under applicable Nigerian law.

10.3  CADE Esports reserves the right to notify the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the EFCC, the NFIU, and any other relevant regulatory authority of findings against an organisation arising from the conduct of its registered player.

10.4  An organisation that knowingly directed, encouraged, financially incentivised, or concealed a player’s integrity violation shall be treated as a co-respondent in any disciplinary or legal proceeding, and may be permanently banned from registering players in any CADE Esports competition.

10.5  The organisation representative signing Section E(c) of this Agreement warrants on behalf of the organisation that: (a) the Organisation-Player Contract submitted to CADE is genuine and current; (b) the organisation is not aware of any integrity violations by the Participant; and (c) the organisation will immediately notify CADE Esports if it becomes aware of any conduct by the Participant that may constitute a breach of this Agreement or the Integrity Policy.

ARTICLE 11: GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1  This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

11.2  Any dispute arising from this Agreement that is not resolved through the internal IDC process or appeals process shall be referred to binding arbitration under the rules of the Lagos Court of Arbitration.

11.3  The parties waive any right to bring claims in public courts except where required by law.
