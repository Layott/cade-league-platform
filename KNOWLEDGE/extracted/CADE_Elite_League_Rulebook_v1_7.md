This document is the binding official rulebook for the CADE Esports eSoccer League: Division 1 (Elite League).All registered players, coaches, and affiliated organisations are required to read, understand,and comply with every rule herein. Ignorance of any rule is not an acceptable defence.

# PART ONE: ORGANISATION & GOVERNANCE

## 1.1 About CADE Esports

CADE Esports is the organising body responsible for the administration, execution, and integrity of the CADE Esports eSoccer League and all associated competitions. The League is operated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and governed by these Official Rules and Regulations.

## 1.2 Governing Bodies

CADE Esports Board of Directors: Final authority on all structural, financial, and constitutional matters.

League Operations Committee (LOC): Day-to-day administration, scheduling, and logistics.

Integrity & Disciplinary Committee (IDC): All matters relating to conduct, match integrity, and sanctions.

Technical Committee: Hardware compliance, in-game settings verification, and technical disputes.

Registration & Compliance Committee: Player and organisation registration.

## 1.3 Official Game & Mode

The official game for Division 1 is EA SPORTS FC (EAFC), the most current edition active at the time of each season. The specific edition will be confirmed in the Season Announcement document published before each season. Division 1 is played in FUT (Ultimate Team) mode combined with Direct Competitive play settings, as specified by the Technical Committee.

# PART TWO: REGISTRATION & ELIGIBILITY

## 2.1 General Eligibility

To be eligible for Division 1, a player must satisfy ALL of the following baseline criteria:

Be a natural person of any age. Players under the age of 18 must submit a completed CADE Parental/Guardian Consent Form signed by a parent or legal guardian as part of their registration.

Hold a valid Nigerian identity document (NIN, National ID, Voter’s Card, International Passport, or Driver’s Licence).

Possess a valid, active EA account in good standing with no active bans or restrictions, a registered CADE App account, and a valid PlayStation Network (PSN) ID. All three must be submitted by the registration deadline (23rd), before the tournament starts.

Have not been previously banned by CADE Esports or any recognised esports governing body for integrity violations, unless formally reinstated.

Complete and submit the official CADE Player Registration Form with all required documentation.

Sign the CADE Esports Player Registration Agreement & Code of Conduct.

## 2.2 Esports Organisation Affiliation

The organisation must be a legally registered business entity in Nigeria with a current CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission) registration number.

The CAC Certificate of Incorporation must be submitted to the Registration & Compliance Committee by the 23rd (2 days before the season start date of the 25th).

The organisation must submit official signed copies of all contracts they hold with their registered player to the Registration & Compliance Committee. Unsigned or incomplete contracts will not be accepted.

An organisation may register a maximum of THREE (3) players in Division 1 per season.

The organisation must have a designated representative or manager as the primary CADE contact.

The organisation must not be under sanction or investigation by CADE, any Nigerian government body, or any internationally recognised esports governing body.

## 2.3 Coach and Manager Requirement

A registered coach is no longer compulsory for Division 1 players. However, a Team Manager from the player’s registered organisation MUST be present at the venue on every match day. If a player has no registered coach, the Team Manager stands in and takes on the courtside support role during matches.

Each player who chooses to register a coach must nominate their Head Coach at the time of registration via the Coach Registration Form. Players without a coach must instead nominate the Team Manager from their organisation using the same form.

A registered coach must be signed to the same organisation as the player(s) they manage. A coach may represent a maximum of THREE (3) Division 1 players at any time, provided all players are signed to the same organisation as the coach.

The identity of the coach or stand-in Team Manager must be verified (valid Nigerian ID required).

The registered coach (or Team Manager standing in) may be seated beside the player throughout the entire match and is permitted to provide verbal strategic support at any point during the match.

If a registered coach or Team Manager becomes unavailable mid-season, the player must notify the LOC within 48 hours and nominate a replacement within 7 days. A player cannot compete without either a coach or Team Manager present on match day.

## 2.4 Caution Fee (Organisations)

All registered organisations must pay a caution fee of ₦50,000 (Fifty Thousand Naira) before the league commences. Payment details are set out in the table below.

The caution fee must be paid by the 24th of April (one day before the league commences on the 25th). Failure to pay by this deadline will result in the organisation’s players being ineligible to compete until payment is confirmed.

Proof of payment must be submitted to the Registration & Compliance Committee alongside other registration documents.

# PART THREE: COMPETITION FORMAT & MATCH RULES

## 3.1 League Format

## 3.2 Venue Arrival & Match Scheduling

SATURDAY match days: All players must be at the official venue by 9:00 AM.

SUNDAY match days: All players must be at the official venue by 12:00 PM (noon).

Match officials will call players to the stage as matches are scheduled throughout the day. Players must be readily available at all times once the match day begins.

The schedule for each match day will be communicated by the LOC at least 12 hours before the match day begins (via the official communications channel).

## 3.3 Equipment Requirements (BYOD)

Console: PlayStation 5 (PS5) ONLY. No other console will be accepted.

Controller/Pad: Any officially licensed PS5 controller. Third-party pads must be declared and approved by the Technical Committee before the season starts.

All peripherals: Players must bring their own earbuds/headsets, Type-C charging cables, and any other accessories they require to play.

The production team provides display monitors and internet connection at the venue only. No other equipment will be supplied.

Equipment will be inspected by the Technical Committee before each match day.

A player who arrives without the required equipment forfeits all scheduled matches for that match day.

## 3.4 Match Day Conduct

### 3.4.1 Phone & Device Policy

No calls, messages, or internet browsing is permitted during an active match.

Failure to surrender a phone when called to the stage will result in the match being delayed and a formal warning issued. Repeated refusal will be treated as a forfeit.

### 3.4.2 In-Match Conduct

Rule 3.4.2.1: Players must report to the stage area promptly when called by an official. A 5-minute grace period applies from the time of calling. After 5 minutes without appearance, a 3-0 forfeit is declared in favour of the opponent.

Rule 3.4.2.2: Players are prohibited from communicating with opponents in any manner intended to distract, demean, intimidate, or harass during the match.

Rule 3.4.2.3: Pausing: The FUT game mode has a built-in, limited pause allowance accessible through the in-game pause queue system. Players must use this system as intended. Attempting to abuse, exploit, or manipulate the pause mechanism beyond its intended function is prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.

Rule 3.4.2.4: Match Settings and Half-time: All matches are played using the Competitive Preset (mandatory). Halves are 9 minutes each. Players are allocated a maximum of THREE (3) minutes at half-time before the second half must commence. A player who exceeds this break without an official reason may be penalised.

Rule 3.4.2.4a: Pre-Match Setup: Players are allocated exactly 5 minutes of setup time before kick-off. Setup covers controller pairing, headset connection, monitor adjustment, and squad confirmation. Any player not ready at the end of the 5 minute window is subject to the late arrival provisions in Rule 3.4.2.1.

Rule 3.4.2.4b: In-Game Team Name: Every player must set their in-game FUT club name to their own registered player name (as submitted in the Player Registration Form). Club names not matching the registered player name will be flagged at equipment inspection and the player must correct the name before play commences.

Rule 3.4.2.5: Players must not use any exploits, bugs, glitches, or in-game mechanics, whether or not they appear on the CADE Banned Mechanics List, that confer an unfair in-game advantage. The IDC has full discretion to rule any mechanic as prohibited.

Rule 3.4.2.6: Any abusive, threatening, or disrespectful language directed at opponents, coaches, officials, or spectators constitutes a conduct violation.

### 3.4.3 Disconnections & Technical Interruptions

Rule 3.4.3.1: Rule (a) Replay: If a disconnection occurs within the first 5 minutes of play AND no goal has been scored by either player at the time of disconnection, the match shall be replayed in full from kick-off.

Rule 3.4.3.2: Rule (b) Restart: In all other cases of involuntary disconnection caused by a technical fault outside the player’s control (e.g., venue power outage, ISP failure, platform crash), whether within the first 5 minutes with a goal already scored or at any time after 5 minutes, play shall be resumed from the exact point of disconnection, maintaining the score and game time at the moment of interruption. The match official will supervise the restart.

Rule 3.4.3.3: Rule (c) Automatic Forfeit: If a player deliberately terminates or exits a match at their own discretion without the express authorisation of the match official (referee), that player is deemed to have automatically forfeited the match 3-0, regardless of the in-game score at the time of the exit. There is no appeal on this ruling.

### 3.4.4 Forfeits

Rule 3.4.4.1: A match is declared a 3-0 forfeit in favour of the non-forfeiting player if the forfeiting player: (a) fails to appear within the 5-minute grace period after being called; (b) deliberately disconnects without official authorisation; (c) is disqualified from that match by a ruling of the IDC; or (d) concedes a voluntary forfeit.

Rule 3.4.4.2: A player who records ONE (1) unexcused forfeit in a season will receive a formal written warning. A SECOND unexcused forfeit results in automatic suspension from all future matches in the season and an immediate IDC referral for review and potential season disqualification. When a player is suspended under this rule, their previously played match results stand unchanged in the league table, but all remaining scheduled matches involving the suspended player are voided and do not count toward any player’s points, goal difference, or other standings metrics.

Rule 3.4.4.3: Forfeits involving no-shows preceding suspicious betting patterns will be automatically referred to the IDC for integrity investigation.

### 3.4.5 Rescheduling

Rule 3.4.5.1: There are NO standard reschedule entitlements. Matches are played on the scheduled match day.

Rule 3.4.5.2: A reschedule may only be considered in very rare, exceptional, and documented circumstances (e.g., verified hospitalisation, declared national emergency, or platform-wide outage). The requesting player must submit written documentation to the LOC as soon as possible, and both players must consent. The LOC’s decision is final.

Rule 3.4.5.3: Rescheduling for personal convenience, travel, social commitments, or vague illness without medical documentation will not be approved.

# PART FOUR: ROSTER & SQUAD MANAGEMENT

## 4.1 Weekly Roster Submission

Rosters must be submitted via a Futbin screenshot showing all player names and player values, sent to the official CADE communications channel designated by the LOC. Futbin is the required submission method. In-app FUT screenshots will not be accepted in place of a Futbin screenshot.

Late submission (after 10:00 AM Thursday), first offence: formal written warning.

Second consecutive late submission: match forfeit for that weekend’s first scheduled match.

Third consecutive failure: IDC review and possible suspension.

The roster submitted on Thursday is the squad the player MUST use for that weekend’s matches. Post-submission changes are only permitted through the Friday Change Window (see Section 4.2).

## 4.2 Friday Evening Change Window

The player wishing to make a change must inform a designated referee or LOC official BEFORE making the substitution in-game.

The referee must authorise and log the change before it is applied.

Only ONE (1) player substitution is permitted per change window per team. Multiple changes in a single window are not permitted.

Changes made outside this window or without referee authorisation are a violation and may result in the player being required to revert to their Thursday roster.

There is no change window for weeks where there is no match day that weekend.

## 4.3 FUT Squad Restrictions

The following restrictions apply to every player’s Ultimate Team squad submitted for Division 1 match play. Squads that fail any of these checks will not be permitted to compete and the player will be liable for forfeit under Rule 3.4.4.1.

Budget cap: Every squad must be built within a maximum budget of 10 Million FUT coins. Squads valued above this cap will be rejected at roster verification.

Nigerian player item: Every squad must contain at least one (1) Nigerian player item. A squad without a Nigerian player is non-compliant and will not be eligible for match play.

Minimum starting minutes: The Nigerian player item must be in the starting XI and must remain on the pitch for at least the first 45 minutes of every match. Substituting the Nigerian player before the 45-minute mark is a rules violation and may result in the match being voided and re-awarded to the opponent.

Goalkeeper budget exemption: The value of the Goalkeeper is NOT included in the 10 Million FUT coin budget calculation. Players may still only use the goalkeeper(s) in the roster submitted on Thursday.

Banned items: Evolution Players (EVOs), Season Pass players, and Objective players are strictly PROHIBITED. Any squad containing one or more of these items will be rejected at roster verification.

Icons and Heroes: Allowed with no quantity limit, provided the total squad value (excluding the Goalkeeper) is within the 10 Million coin budget.

# PART FIVE: PLAYER OBLIGATIONS

## 5.1 Attendance

Players must be available and present on ALL scheduled match days throughout the entire season.

Absence without advance notice and documented reason is a breach of the Player Agreement and may be referred to the IDC.

A player unable to attend a match day must notify the LOC as early as possible and provide supporting documentation within 24 hours.

## 5.2 Social Media & League Promotion

Every Division 1 player MUST post at least ONCE per week on a minimum of TWO (2) of the following platforms: Twitter/X, Instagram, or TikTok.

Posts must positively promote the CADE Esports eSoccer League and actively encourage the public to watch, follow, or attend.

Posts must reference or tag the official CADE Esports account. Specific hashtags will be provided in the Season Briefing.

Players may be asked to submit screenshots of their posts as proof at any time during the season.

## 5.3 Dress Code & Presentation

All players MUST wear their official team/organisation jersey to every match. Jerseys are mandatory matchday attire and must be worn for the duration of all scheduled matches. Players arriving without a jersey will not be permitted to compete until one is provided.

Clothing promoting illegal activity, offensive imagery, or content that brings the League into disrepute is prohibited.

A match official may refuse entry to any player not meeting dress standards.

## 5.4 Punctuality & Late Arrival

Players must be checked in with the LOC representative at the venue by the mandatory arrival time (Saturday: 9:00 AM | Sunday: 12:00 PM). Being physically in the building but not checked in does not count.

## 5.5 Match Day Content Obligations

The production team will allocate content sessions (interviews, pieces to camera, on-brand shoots) to each player on league match days. Participation is a contractual obligation and forms part of the player’s registration.

Every player will receive a designated content slot on the relevant match day, communicated in advance by the production team.

A player who cannot attend their allocated match-day content slot must instead attend a makeup session on any day between Tuesday and Thursday of the following week, at the specific time set by the production team.

Makeup session attendance is the player’s own responsibility. Transport, scheduling and venue arrival are all on the player.

Missing both the match-day content slot AND the Tuesday to Thursday makeup session without documented reason will result in a deduction of ONE (1) league point for that week.

## 5.6 Unauthorized Access to Restricted Areas

The production set, backstage areas, control room, and any other production or technical area marked as restricted are off-limits to players unless they have been expressly invited by a member of the production team or an LOC official.

Any player who forces their way onto the set or into any restricted production area when not authorised will be fined ₦25,000. The fine is deducted from the registered organisation’s caution fee. The organisation is required to top the caution fee back up to ₦50,000 within 7 days of the deduction or the player becomes ineligible to compete until payment is confirmed.

Repeat offences may be referred to the IDC for further sanction on top of the fine.

# PART SIX: INTEGRITY & CONDUCT

All integrity and anti-match fixing matters are governed in full by the CADE Esports Integrity, Anti-Match Fixing & Betting Policy (the “Integrity Policy”), which is a separate but legally binding document forming part of the overall regulatory framework. This section provides a summary only. Players are required to have read the full Integrity Policy before signing the Player Agreement.

## 6.1 General Conduct Standards

All participants must treat fellow players, coaches, officials, staff, and spectators with respect and dignity at all times.

Public communications (social media, press statements, interviews) must not bring the League into disrepute.

Players must not engage in or publicly promote discrimination of any kind.

Players must not consume alcohol, cigarettes, smoke (including vaping), or any controlled or recreational substances (edible or otherwise) BEFORE or DURING any official CADE match event or at the venue on match days.

## 6.2 Betting Prohibitions

Players must not place any bet on any match in which they are a participant.

Players must not use inside information to place bets on any CADE League match.

Players must not facilitate, encourage, or assist any third party in placing bets connected to corrupt arrangements.

Any corrupt approach received from a third party must be reported to the IDC within 24 hours.

## 6.3 Sanctions Overview

## 6.4 Appeals

Any sanctioned player may appeal within 5 business days of the written decision.

Appeals must be submitted in writing to the CADE Esports Board of Directors.

An Appeal Panel of 3 independent members will review the appeal.

The Appeal Panel’s decision is final and binding.
