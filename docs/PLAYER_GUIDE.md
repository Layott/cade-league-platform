# CADE Elite League — Player Guide

Welcome to the **CADE Esports Pro League — Division 1 Elite (2025-2026 Season)**. This guide walks you through everything you need to know to use the league website.

---

## 1. Login

**Site:** https://cade-league.vercel.app

**Your account:**
- **Email** — `<your-gamertag>@cade.local` (e.g. `mr.oga@cade.local`, `faruk@cade.local`).
- **Password** — sent to you privately by the league admin. Keep it safe.

**Steps:**
1. Open https://cade-league.vercel.app in any browser (mobile or desktop).
2. Click **Sign in** (top-right).
3. Enter your email + password.
4. You land on your **player dashboard** at `/player`.

If you forget your password, message the admin — they can reset it for you.

---

## 2. Your Dashboard — `/player`

When you sign in, you see:

- **Squad due** — countdown to the next weekend's submission deadline.
- **Open cases** — any disciplinary or appeal item that needs your attention.
- **Recent notifications** — match results, admin announcements, squad-window opens, etc.

The top nav lets you jump to: **Squad**, **Cases**, **Disputes**, **Appeals**, **Profile**.

---

## 3. Submit Your Squad — `/player/squad`

This is the most important page each week.

### How it works

- The league plays one squad per **weekend** (Sat + Sun count as one weekend, sharing the same squad).
- The 4 weekends remaining this season:
  - **May 2-3** (Sat-Sun)
  - **May 9-10** (Sat-Sun)
  - **May 16-17** (Sat-Sun)
  - **May 23** (final weekend)
- Submission window opens **Saturday** of the previous week and closes **Thursday 10:00 WAT** the morning of the matches.
- After Thursday 10:00 WAT, you can no longer submit. There is a brief **Friday change window** (21:00 — 22:00 WAT) for late edits — admin announces this each week.

### Steps

1. Click **Squad** in the top nav (or go to `/player/squad`).
2. You'll see your weekend pickers grouped by date (e.g. *Weekend · May 2-3*). Click the open one.
3. Two ways to submit:
   - **Picker mode** — search for players in the league's price database (FCDB), pick 11 starters + however many subs your **10M coin** budget allows, place them on the pitch.
   - **Screenshot mode** — upload a clean Futbin screenshot of your full squad (rating, position, club, nation visible). The admin transcribes it.
4. Click **Submit squad**. You'll see a confirmation + the page refreshes to show *Submitted · Pending validation*.
5. The admin validates your squad within 24h. You'll see one of:
   - **Approved** — your squad is locked in for the weekend.
   - **Rejected** — submission was rejected (rule violation). The reason is shown; you may resubmit before deadline.

### Squad rules — must be valid to be approved

- **Total budget ≤ 10,000,000 coins** — sum of FCDB price for every player you put in.
- **Minimum 1 Nigerian item** — at least one player on your final squad must be Nigerian.
- **Banned item types** — no EVO cards, Season Pass cards, or Objective cards. Only normal / special / hero / icon / TOTS / TOTY / RTTF cards are allowed.
- **You may have any number of subs**, as long as the budget rule is respected.
- **One squad per weekend** — submitting for Saturday automatically applies to Sunday.

### After submission

- **Before deadline**: you can resubmit; the new submission overwrites the old.
- **After deadline**: you cannot edit unless the admin grants a Friday change window or manually re-opens.

## 4. View / Edit During the Friday Change Window — `/player/squad/change`

If the admin opens the Friday 21:00-22:00 WAT change window, you'll see a banner on `/player/squad` linking to `/player/squad/change`. You may swap **at most 1 player** in this window. Use it sparingly — abuse can be flagged.

## 5. Profile — `/profile`

Manage your own profile:

- **Display name + gamer tag** — visible on broadcasts + standings.
- **Avatar** — your headshot. Used on the broadcast overlay.
- **Email + password** — change password any time.
- **Bank details** (optional) — used for prize payouts. Visible only to admin.

If you change your gamer tag, please notify the admin so the broadcast overlay's photo lookup stays in sync.

---

## 6. View Cases — `/player/cases`

If you receive a disciplinary action (warning, point deduction, fine, suspension), it appears on this page. Each case shows:

- **Type** of incident (e.g. late attendance, no-show, conduct).
- **Severity + sanction** applied.
- **Decided at** + **expires at** dates.
- **Summary** — the admin's note explaining the case.
- **Appeal status** — whether you've appealed + the outcome.

If you disagree with a sanction, you can **file an appeal** (see §8 below).

---

## 7. Raise a Dispute — `/player/disputes/new`

If a match result is disputed (wrong score entered, server crash, opponent no-show), file a dispute within **24 hours of the match**.

**Steps:**
1. Click **Disputes** → **New dispute**.
2. Fill out:
   - **Match day + opponent** — auto-resolved.
   - **Type** — score-mismatch / no-show / server-issue / other.
   - **Title** + **Description** — what happened, when, what should the score be.
   - **Evidence** — upload screenshots / videos (max 50MB per file).
3. Submit.
4. Watch your dashboard for an admin response. The admin can: ACCEPT the dispute (re-record the result), REJECT it, or PARTIALLY ACCEPT (e.g. award draw).

---

## 8. File an Appeal — `/player/appeals/new`

If you receive a sanction you think is unfair:

**Steps:**
1. Click **Appeals** → **New appeal**.
2. Pick the **case** you're appealing.
3. Write your **statement** — why the sanction should be reversed or reduced.
4. Attach **evidence** if you have it.
5. Submit.

The admin reviews + decides: UPHELD (sanction stands), OVERTURNED (sanction removed), or REDUCED.

You'll see the outcome on your `/player/appeals` page + receive a notification.

## 11. Tips + Best Practices

- **Submit early.** The admin has more time to flag rule violations + you can resubmit before the deadline. Don't wait until Thursday 09:55.
- **Use the picker mode if you can** — the system pre-validates your budget + bans live as you build. Screenshot mode requires manual transcription which delays validation.
- **Keep evidence.** For disputes, screenshots + match recordings are decisive. Video is best.
- **Check notifications daily** during weekends. Admin pings + sanction notices land here first.
- **Profile + photo** — keep your headshot up to date. It's used on the broadcast overlay during your matches.

2. Note the URL.
3. Message the league admin with both.

The admin can check audit logs to see exactly what your account did + restore state if needed.
